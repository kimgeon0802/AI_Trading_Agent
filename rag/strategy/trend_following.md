# Trend Following

## Core Principle
Trend following is an investment strategy that seeks to make profit from market momentum by executing trades based on the direction of a market trend.

## Analysis Relevance
- Provides a systematic approach to riding market trends.

## Decision Guidelines
- Define trends using indicators like moving averages or price action.
- Only enter when a clear trend is established; avoid trying to guess the start of a trend.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Consistent profit-taking during prolonged market trends confirms trend-following effectiveness.

## Risk Signals
- Trend following often involves many 'whipsaws' or false signals; improper risk management during these periods can be costly.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Trying to catch the very bottom or top of a trend.
- Believing trend following can predict the *future* trend.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate trend direction.
- Do NOT claim a trend exists without looking at the provided chart data.
