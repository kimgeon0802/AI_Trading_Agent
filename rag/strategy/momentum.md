# Momentum

## Core Principle
Momentum investing focuses on capitalizing on the continuation of existing market trends. Stocks that are rising tend to continue rising, and stocks that are falling tend to continue falling.

## Analysis Relevance
- Use to capture trends when market direction is clear.

## Decision Guidelines
- Enter positions when momentum is strong and confirmed by other indicators (like volume).
- Exit when momentum begins to fade or reverses.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- High momentum scores in stocks with solid fundamentals often indicate strong trends.

## Risk Signals
- Buying at the peak of a momentum cycle without risk management leads to significant losses when the trend reverses.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Assuming momentum works in all market environments (it can fail in sideways markets).
- Confusing high momentum with high fundamental value.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate 'momentum_score' data.
- Do NOT predict trend continuation without evidence.
