# Warren Buffett

## Core Principle
Warren Buffett emphasizes long-term value, competitive advantage (moat), and disciplined allocation into great businesses.

## Analysis Relevance
- Framework for identifying high-quality companies that are built to last.

## Decision Guidelines
- Focus on companies with durable competitive advantages (moats).
- Be patient; holding time is a key factor in compounding.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Businesses with consistent, high returns on invested capital (ROIC) over long periods often fit Buffett's criteria.

## Risk Signals
- Investing in commodity-like businesses without moats during industry downturns.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Believing Buffett never sells stocks (he sells when the thesis changes).
- Focusing solely on current low prices rather than company quality.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate the existence of a 'moat' for a company.
- Do NOT attribute investment decisions to Buffett without solid data.
