# Howard Marks

## Core Principle
Howard Marks emphasizes understanding market cycles, risk management, and the difference between price and value, rather than trying to predict the future.

## Analysis Relevance
- Guides the agent in maintaining a long-term perspective during market volatility.

## Decision Guidelines
- Focus on the 'second-level thinking'—not just what the market thinks, but what others might miss.
- Respect market cycles: buy when others are fearful, sell when others are greedy.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Strategies that perform well during downturns demonstrate superior risk management and 'second-level' understanding.

## Risk Signals
- Ignoring the stage of the market cycle is a major risk, according to Marks' philosophy.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Trying to time the exact top or bottom of a cycle.
- Believing market predictions are accurate or reliable.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate market cycle stages.
- Do NOT make investment decisions based on Marks' quotes without contextual data.
