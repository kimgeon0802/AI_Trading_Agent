# Peter Lynch

## Core Principle
Peter Lynch advocates for investing in what you know, performing thorough bottom-up research, and maintaining a long-term outlook.

## Analysis Relevance
- Useful for individual stock research based on qualitative understanding of business models.

## Decision Guidelines
- Research companies whose products you understand.
- Look for growth potential at a reasonable price (GARP).

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Consistent long-term growth and stable earnings support Lynch-style 'story' stocks.

## Risk Signals
- Investing in 'story' stocks without solid fundamentals leads to high risk of permanent capital loss.

## Common Misinterpretations
- 'Buy what you know' does not mean buy without research.
- Ignoring fundamental financial health.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate 'Lynch-style' stories for stocks.
- Do NOT attribute stock picks to Lynch without evidence.
