# Benjamin Graham

## Core Principle
Benjamin Graham, the father of value investing, emphasized rigorous fundamental analysis, focus on margin of safety, and long-term perspective.

## Analysis Relevance
- Provides the foundational framework for value-based screening.

## Decision Guidelines
- Focus on companies with solid balance sheets.
- Seek a substantial 'margin of safety' between market price and intrinsic value.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Companies exhibiting 'net-net' characteristics or consistently trading below asset value often meet Graham's criteria.

## Risk Signals
- Neglecting the margin of safety leads to speculative risk, contrary to Graham's philosophy.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Assuming value investing is synonymous with buying 'dying' companies.
- Failing to differentiate between price and value.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT attribute investment decisions to Graham without data.
- Do NOT fabricate 'Graham-number' calculations.
