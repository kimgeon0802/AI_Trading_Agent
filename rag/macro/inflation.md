# Inflation

## Core Principle
Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services is rising. It erodes purchasing power over time.

## Analysis Relevance
- High inflation impacts consumer spending behavior and raises corporate input costs.

## Decision Guidelines
- Evaluate whether companies have the pricing power to pass on increased costs to consumers.
- Use inflation data to gauge the likelihood of central bank interest rate hikes.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Companies maintaining stable margins during periods of high inflation often possess strong moats.

## Risk Signals
- Persistent, high inflation is a significant risk for companies with low margins or low pricing power.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Assuming moderate inflation is always negative (it can be a sign of a growing economy).
- Failing to differentiate between cost-push and demand-pull inflation.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate CPI data or inflation rates.
- Do NOT predict future inflation trends.
