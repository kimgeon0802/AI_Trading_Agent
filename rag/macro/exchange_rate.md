# Exchange Rate

## Core Principle
The exchange rate is the value of one currency for the purpose of conversion to another. It significantly impacts companies involved in international trade.

## Analysis Relevance
- For export-oriented economies (e.g., Korea), exchange rate fluctuations directly impact corporate earnings and competitiveness.

## Decision Guidelines
- Monitor the trend of major currency pairs relevant to the stocks being analyzed.
- Avoid overreacting to short-term currency volatility; focus on sustained trends.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Exporters showing resilience despite strong domestic currency often indicate strong pricing power or diversified revenue streams.

## Risk Signals
- Rapid, unexpected depreciation of domestic currency increases import costs and inflationary pressure, posing risks to many sectors.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Assuming a weaker currency is always beneficial for all exporters.
- Ignoring the impact of hedging strategies used by companies.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT predict future exchange rate movements.
- Do NOT fabricate the impact of exchange rates on specific stocks without analysis.
