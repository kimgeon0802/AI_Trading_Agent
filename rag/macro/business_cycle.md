# Business Cycle

## Core Principle
The business cycle represents the natural fluctuation of the economy between periods of expansion and contraction (recession).

## Analysis Relevance
- Market performance is highly correlated with the current stage of the business cycle (early, mid, late expansion, or recession).

## Decision Guidelines
- Adjust asset allocation and sector exposure based on the current cycle stage.
- Do not apply defensive strategies during the peak of an expansion.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Cyclical stocks performing strongly at the start of an expansion validate the business cycle analysis.

## Risk Signals
- Late-cycle indicators (high inflation, rising rates) signal increased recession risk and the need to derisk.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Predicting the exact timing or duration of the business cycle.
- Assuming the cycle is obsolete.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate the current business cycle stage.
- Do NOT predict recession start dates.
