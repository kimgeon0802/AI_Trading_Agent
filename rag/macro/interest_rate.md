# Interest Rate

## Core Principle
Interest rates are the cost of borrowing money. They are the primary tool central banks use to control inflation and economic activity.

## Analysis Relevance
- Higher rates increase discount rates (lowering present value of future cash flows) and raise borrowing costs.

## Decision Guidelines
- Be cautious with highly leveraged companies when rates are rising.
- Do not assume rate hikes will immediately cause stock market crashes.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Financial institutions often perform better in a rising-rate environment.

## Risk Signals
- Rapid, unanticipated rate hikes can trigger significant market corrections.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Believing that interest rates have a uniform effect on all sectors.
- Assuming rate changes are the only factor driving market performance.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT predict future interest rate decisions.
- Do NOT fabricate the effect of rate changes on a specific stock.
