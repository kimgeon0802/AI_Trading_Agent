# Confirmation Bias

## Core Principle
Confirmation bias is the tendency to search for, interpret, and recall information in a way that confirms one's pre-existing beliefs or hypotheses.

## Analysis Relevance
- A major risk when the AI agent forms an initial decision and then only searches for data to support it.

## Decision Guidelines
- Actively seek out information that *contradicts* the current hypothesis/decision.
- If the agent is leaning toward 'BUY', force it to generate a 'SELL' case.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Explicit consideration of contrarian viewpoints validates the robustness of the decision-making process.

## Risk Signals
- Ignoring negative news while fixating on positive reports is a classic sign of confirmation bias leading to bad decisions.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Believing that "having a lot of information" prevents confirmation bias (it can actually facilitate it).
- Confusing confirmation bias with conviction.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate supporting news items to confirm a decision.
- Do NOT dismiss contradicting data as 'noise' without investigation.
