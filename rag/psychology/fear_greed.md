# Fear and Greed

## Core Principle
Fear and greed are the two primary emotions that drive investor behavior. Excessive fear leads to panic selling; excessive greed leads to speculative buying.

## Analysis Relevance
- Understand that extreme market moves are often driven by these emotions, not fundamentals.

## Decision Guidelines
- When the market is driven by extreme fear, look for undervalued opportunities.
- When the market is driven by extreme greed, look for overvaluation and risk.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Strategies that remain objective during periods of extreme fear and greed tend to outperform.

## Risk Signals
- Extreme greed is a strong risk signal indicating a potential top; extreme fear is a risk signal indicating a potential bottom (or further crash).

## Common Misinterpretations
- Trying to 'time the market' perfectly based on sentiment.
- Assuming fear and greed indicators are precise market signals.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate "Fear & Greed Index" numbers.
- Do NOT attribute current market behavior to "investor greed" without evidence.
