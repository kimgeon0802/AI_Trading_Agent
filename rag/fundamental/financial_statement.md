# Financial Statement

## Core Principle
Financial statements (Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow) provide a snapshot of a company's financial health, operational efficiency, and liquidity.

## Analysis Relevance
- Use to assess a company's ability to generate profit and manage debt.
- Look for trends in revenue growth and margin stability.

## Decision Guidelines
- Compare financial metrics against historical performance and industry peers.
- Do not make decisions based on a single quarter's data; look for consistent long-term performance.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Consistent revenue growth and debt reduction support value-investing strategies.

## Risk Signals
- Declining operating margins and rising debt-to-equity ratios are major risk signals.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Assuming high revenue growth always translates to high profitability.
- Ignoring cash flow statement in favor of reported net income.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate financial statement figures (e.g., revenue, net income).
- Do NOT compute ratios (e.g., ROE) without explicit input data.
