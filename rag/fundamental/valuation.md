# Valuation

## Core Principle
Valuation is the process of determining the current worth of an asset. It involves evaluating a company's fundamentals relative to its industry and historical performance.

## Analysis Relevance
- Critical for determining the risk-reward profile of an entry.

## Decision Guidelines
- Prefer stocks with low valuation multiples relative to industry peers (e.g., PER, PBR).
- Valuation should be combined with qualitative analysis, not used in isolation.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Consistent valuation multiples over time can suggest a stable, mature company suitable for value investing.

## Risk Signals
- Extreme valuation premiums without corresponding growth suggest speculative bubbles.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Using a single metric (e.g., PER) to define the entire valuation.
- Comparing valuation multiples across entirely different industries.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate valuation ratios (PER, PBR).
- Do NOT make market-wide valuation claims based on single-stock analysis.
