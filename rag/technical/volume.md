# Volume

## Core Principle
Trading volume is the number of shares of a security traded during a given period. It confirms the strength or weakness of a price trend.

## Analysis Relevance
- High volume validates price moves; low volume indicates weak conviction.

## Decision Guidelines
- A price increase on high volume indicates strong buying interest.
- A price decrease on low volume indicates weak selling interest.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Trends that show increasing volume as the price moves in the trend direction are considered strong.

## Risk Signals
- Price movements on low volume are often unreliable and prone to reversals.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Interpreting volume spikes without understanding the context (e.g., earnings announcement).
- Believing high volume automatically means institutional buying.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate volume data.
- Do NOT claim institutional action based solely on volume.
