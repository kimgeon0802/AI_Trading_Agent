# Moving Average

## Core Principle
A moving average is a technical indicator that smooths out price data by creating a constantly updated average price to help identify trends.

## Analysis Relevance
- Helps filter out market noise and identify the overall trend direction.

## Decision Guidelines
- Use short-term moving averages for short-term trends and long-term averages for major trend identification.
- Watch for crossovers between different moving averages.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Consistent price support at major moving averages (like the 200-day) validates the trend's strength.

## Risk Signals
- Prices consistently closing below long-term moving averages indicate a bearish trend.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Believing moving averages predict future price action.
- Using them without considering current market volatility.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate moving average values.
- Do NOT claim a 'Golden Cross' or 'Dead Cross' occurred without data.
