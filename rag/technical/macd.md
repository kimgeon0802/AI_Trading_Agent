# MACD

## Core Principle
The Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) is a trend-following momentum indicator that shows the relationship between two moving averages of a security’s price.

## Analysis Relevance
- Used to identify changes in momentum or trend direction.

## Decision Guidelines
- Watch for crossover signals between the MACD line and the signal line.
- Do not rely solely on MACD without trend confirmation.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- MACD crossovers that align with significant price trends support the validity of the signal.

## Risk Signals
- MACD can produce false signals in sideways or low-volatility markets.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Believing MACD crossovers are guaranteed entry/exit signals.
- Confusing the MACD line with price action.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate MACD values.
- Do NOT claim a crossover happened if the data does not support it.
