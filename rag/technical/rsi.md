# RSI

## Core Principle
The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is a momentum indicator that measures the magnitude of recent price changes to evaluate overbought or oversold conditions.

## Analysis Relevance
- Helps identify potential reversal points.

## Decision Guidelines
- RSI above 70 is typically considered overbought; below 30 is oversold.
- RSI signals are most reliable in range-bound markets.

## Strategy Validation Signals
- Price reversals after RSI hits extreme levels support the RSI indicator's validity.

## Risk Signals
- Strong trends can keep RSI at overbought or oversold levels for extended periods, leading to early reversals.

## Common Misinterpretations
- Assuming overbought (RSI > 70) means the price *must* fall.
- Assuming oversold (RSI < 30) means the price *must* rise.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do NOT fabricate RSI values.
- Do NOT predict a reversal solely because RSI is > 70 or < 30.
